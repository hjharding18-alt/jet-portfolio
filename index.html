B · Independent software product

Golf With Jet. Live scores, zero fuss.

A mobile-first web app for running golf tournaments: players enter scores on their phones and everyone watches a live leaderboard. I own it end to end: product direction, scoring rules, data model, security, testing and releases, building with AI-assisted development and reviewing every change.

Explore details

01

Tournament creation & management Built

Name it, pick a saved course, round length, team size, format, tees and handicap rules, then share a short join code. Admins manage rosters, teams, tee times, co-admins and a printable scorecard. Only approved accounts can create tournaments, and the database enforces it.

02

Scoring formats Built

Six formats: stroke play, scramble, Texas scramble, best ball, net best ball and Stableford. Optional handicaps with 2, 3 and 4-player team formulas, skins with carryovers, and a Drink Handicap side game for the fun rounds.

03

Live leaderboards Built

Everyone enters their own scores on their phone and the board updates in real time. Format-aware totals, projected finish, live versus final boards, and a submit, review and verify flow before results are final.

04

Interactive course maps Built

MapLibre maps on satellite or street basemaps with tees, greens, bunkers and water, plus live front, center and back yardages from your location, shot tracking and a recenter control. Course geometry comes from cited sources such as OpenStreetMap.

05

Authentication & roles Validating

Passwordless magic link and Google sign-in. Owner, co-admin and player roles plus app admin roles, all enforced with Postgres row-level security and server-side functions. Signed-in role testing is part of Phase 9.

06

Offline & installable Partial

Installable as a home-screen app. Scores entered without signal are queued on the device and sync when the connection returns, with a visible sync status. Full offline loading and conflict resolution are on the roadmap.
