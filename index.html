Live leaderboards Built

Realtime updates, format-aware aggregation, live vs. final boards, and a review-and-verify round lifecycle.

Scoring engine Built

Six formats, optional handicaps, 2/3/4-player team handicaps, skins with carryovers, plus a "Drink Handicap" side game.
